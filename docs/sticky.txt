hi! a few quick answers before you post :3

why this exists: fallout 4 draws terminals in your pip-boy colour, and with pip-boy fx off it forces plain white. lots of us turn fx off to lose the flicker, then had no easy way to colour terminals. now you pick a colour and go, no ini edits. to quote our lord and savior todd howard: it just works™

[b]white pip-boy, green terminals?[/b]
set your pip-boy colour the usual way (the game's setting or a mod), then pick the green preset in this mod's MCM. holotapes you play on your pip-boy keep your pip-boy colour, terminals in the world and while hacking use the mod's. want your terminal colour on tapes too? set "Pip-Boy holotapes" to terminal colour. thanks to [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/profile/DimitriDark]DimitriDark[/url] for helping me get that one right.

[b]what is pip-boy fx?[/b]
the bPipboyDisableFX setting in Fallout4Custom.ini. the mod works with it on or off, no change needed.

[b]other terminal colour mods?[/b]
not compatible, use one at a time.

[b]found a bug?[/b]
post in Bugs with this mod's log and your load order. the description says where to find both. reports without them close after 14 days, but post them later and i'll reopen it.
