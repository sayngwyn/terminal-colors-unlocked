# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- New: the small prompt and blinking cursor on a terminal's screen, seen as you walk up to it, now use your colour too instead of vanilla green. Turning the mod off puts the green back.
- Fixed: the settings holotape could be given twice when you started a new game. You now get one. If you already have two, the extra one is harmless.
- The page now explains Pip-Boy FX.

## 1.0.1

- The download now includes the licence (GPL-3.0), a note on where the source is, and the CommonLibF4 licences, in a Licenses folder. Nothing in the game changes.

## 1.0.0

First release.
- Terminal text in your own colour, with Pip-Boy FX on or off.
- MCM page with presets, red, green and blue sliders and a live preview.
- A settings holotape for players without MCM.
- Installer with the right DLL for Fallout 4 1.10.163 / 1.10.984 or 1.11.
